Celebrations

Weddings & special events

Thank you for your interest in planning your wedding or special event at Second Empire.

Second Empire is the perfect setting for an elegant wedding ceremony, in or outdoors, reception, rehearsal dinner and bridal luncheons. We can assist a bride with her photography, flowers, entertainment as well as the food and service to make her day as seamless as possible.

For further information and to make plans, please call Ashley Dyson, our Special Events Coordinator at 919-829-3663. She will gladly offer recommendations in your planning and design a menu for your event. Email: specialevents@second-empire.com.

The Owners, Chef, Management and Staff have combined their efforts, commitment and talents to develop and present for you a special and unforgettable dining experience.

What to expect

  • Corporate gatherings & group occasions
