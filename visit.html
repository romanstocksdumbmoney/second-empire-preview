Reservations

Please call us at 919-829-3663, or make a reservation online. If you have a preference, please specify if you would like to dine in the Upstairs Main Dining Rooms, in the Tavern or in The Atrium.

Due to limited seating, please let us know if you are not able to honor your reservation or if you are running late. If your party has not arrived or called within 15 minutes after your reservation time, the restaurant reserves the right to cancel your reservation.

Parking

Second Empire has several parking spots available at the restaurant, our back Edenton Street Lot. Parking downtown is also free on the streets after 6pm where clearly stated. There are also multiple paid parking lots surrounding the restaurant as well.
